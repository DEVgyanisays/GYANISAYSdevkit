Registration privacy notice

This form supports the Apollo Consultant Forum picnic organised with GyaniSays. It records your RSVP, name, department, age group, preferences, optional health/accessibility notes and accompanying family details.

Authenticated organisers can view registration details to plan attendance, food, activities and assistance. The kids food manifest contains only ages, age bands and food preferences; it excludes names, parent details and medical notes.

Submitting the form saves these details in the event application's database. No email or messaging service is connected. The site does not send analytics events. It may request fonts from Google Fonts where the existing design uses them.

Organiser access uses the website’s protected admin login. Imported names and departments are visible in the public name picker; contact and health details are never included in that list. A temporary submission identifier is stored in your browser's session storage to prevent duplicate submissions during retries. Application data is not stored in browser local storage.

Provide family details only with appropriate permission. You can leave medical and special-care fields blank. Contact the organiser who shared this registration link to request correction or removal. The organiser must establish and communicate the retention period before public launch; automatic deletion is not configured.

This installation runs locally until hosting is arranged. Do not publish it until the organiser has approved this notice, supplied a privacy contact and configured HTTPS and backups.
